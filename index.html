NEON CITY, 1986

Officer Vega answers a break-in call at Chronotech Labs and grabs the strange device on the workbench. One blinding flash later, Vega is standing in ancient Rome with the lab's prototype time drive clipped to the duty belt.

The drive is busted. Its three missing parts are scattered through history, and every army that spots a time-traveling cop wants Vega gone.

Fight through them on foot, recover the parts, and get back to 1986.

Drag anywhere to move (mouse or arrow keys on a computer). Vega fires automatically. Gold badges power up your gun; donuts are bonus points.
