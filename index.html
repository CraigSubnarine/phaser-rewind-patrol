NEON CITY, 1986

Officer Vega answers a break-in call at Chronotech Labs and grabs the strange device on the workbench. One blinding flash later, he's walking down a road somewhere in time with the lab's prototype time drive clipped to his duty belt.

The drive is busted. Its five missing parts are scattered through the past and the future, and every army that spots a time-traveling cop wants him gone.

Every run takes a different path: five timelines out of eleven, from the Stone Age to 3000 AD. Each has two levels of five back-to-back waves, with a boss at the end of every level. Beat a mini-boss and you might get a bonus chase or escape on horseback (or chariot, boat, raptor, hoverbike, starfighter...). Grab the coins enemies drop, gear up at the market, recover the parts, and get back to 1986. Or keep going, and see how long you last.

Drag anywhere to move (mouse or arrow keys on a computer). Vega fires automatically. Donuts heal; coffee from a mid-boss heals fully. Active items charge as you take enemies down: tap the item button (or press Space) when it glows.

Same seed, same timelines and shop rolls. Share it to race a friend on the same run.
